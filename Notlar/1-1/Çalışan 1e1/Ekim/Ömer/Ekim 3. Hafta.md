Phase 1: Arrive Present
Before the meeting, ask yourself:
-How has this person seemed recently?
-What pressure might they be under?
-What would be genuinely helpful for them to talk through today?
-What action did they commit to last time, and how did it go?
 
Phase 2: Explore With Open Questions
Hayat nasıl gidiyor?, "Geçen seferden beri aklını meşgul eden şeyler neler?"

Benzer sorular:
“What’s been taking the most energy recently?”
“What’s feeling harder than it should?”
“What’s something you’re quietly proud of?”
“If nothing changed for six months, what would worry you?”

Doğru anladığından emin ol, sorunu çözmeye çalışma, öneri al ve sonraki adımda neler yapmak gerektiğini sor.
